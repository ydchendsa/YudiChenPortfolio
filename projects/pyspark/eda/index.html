Exploratory Data Analysis

Executive Summary#

In this section, several EDA visualizations are utilized to explore basic information about Reddit users who contribute to Marvel-related Reddit channels and consider themselves Marvel fans. By exploring fan behaviors, we aim to provide insights about fan reactions to Marvel productions during January 2021–August 2022. This period was critical for the MCU as the original Avengers ended their contracts, and new lead characters such as Spider-Man and Doctor Strange emerged. Fan reactions, whether positive or negative, will influence Marvel’s products in the next decades.

Key elements of user behavior analyzed include post and comment lengths, submission times, and post content. Contrary to popular belief, Marvel’s transition from Phase 1 (Avengers) to Phase 2 (Spider-Man and Doctor Strange) has been successful. Discussions have focused on new characters; few people still discuss previous Avengers. The new TV series WandaVision sparked significant discussion in January 2021. Other hot discussions occurred in August 2021, September 2021, June 2022, and August 2022, coinciding with Marvel’s 60th anniversary and new movie releases.

Overall, EDA visualizations indicate that Marvel fans have been engaged with the company’s new productions and characters, highlighting the success of Marvel’s transition during January 2021–August 2022.

Analysis Report#

The data in this and subsequent sections come from the subreddit “Marvel.” Submission data were joined with comment data to create the analytical dataset. Five visualizations are used in further analysis:

  1. Figure 1: Box plot of submission and comment scores (likes minus dislikes), with outliers excluded to avoid bias.
  2. Figure 2: Distribution of submission and comment scores.
  3. Figure 3: Correlation among variables.
  4. Figure 4: Heated discussion days during the analytical period.
  5. Figure 5: Time difference between submissions and comments.

Dummy variables and summary tables reflect discussion intensity across weekdays, reply patterns after submissions, and discussions on different topics.

Original Dataset

Figure 1#

Both comment and submission scores are skewed. Most scores fall in the 0–500 range, reflecting typical engagement on Reddit.

Figure 2#

Post length correlates with scores and the speed at which posts receive comments. Longer posts tend to attract more engagement.

Figure 4#

Heat-map visualization shows more posts are published on weekends. Exceptions in May and August 2022 coincide with the release of Doctor Strange and Thor.

Figure 5#

Comments peak within the first three days after posting. Scores drop after five days, and users generally respond within 0–2 days, when posts reach peak engagement.
