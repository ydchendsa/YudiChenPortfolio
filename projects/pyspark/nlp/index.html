Natural Language Processing

While many might expect Captain America, Spider-Man, or Iron Man to dominate Reddit chatter, analysis shows that Wanda Maximoff (WandaVision) appears most frequently. This spike is likely driven by the Disney+ show WandaVision (early 2021, 6.5M domestic views) and the 2022 film Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, in which Wanda plays a lead role.

The MCU’s ongoing, interconnected storytelling encourages audiences to follow each installment, while a woman-led streaming show broadens the viewer base.

Sentiment analysis over time reveals more negative or low sentiment in the afternoon and before bedtime compared to mornings. Despite this, Disney+ subscriptions grew steadily, particularly during evening hours when Reddit activity peaked — highlighting strong potential for further streaming growth.

Analysis Report#

External Data#

Dataset 1: DIS_stock.csv

  • Period: Jan 2021 – Aug 2022
  • Fields: Open, Close, Volume (Yahoo Finance)

Dataset 2: Marvel_box.csv

  • Fields: title, year, runtime, imdb_rating, director, main cast, domestic/foreign gross revenue

Distribution of Text Lengths#

  • Submissions are generally longer than comments (expected since they start discussions).
  • Users rarely write very long texts.

Most Common Words#

After cleaning (tokenizing, lemmatizing, removing stopwords), top words:
movie, mcu, avenger, spiderman, character, wanda, marvel, show, vision, captain.

Top 10 Words by TF-IDF#


TF-IDF highlights relevance of terms. Highest scores:
wanda, kid, vision, mcu, live, character.

Confirms Wanda/Vision centrality.

K-Means Clustering#


  • Used Word2Vec + Elbow Method → best K ≈ 6.
  • But for hidden topics, LDA (topic modeling) is more effective.

Topic Modeling#


  • LDA finds hidden topics with coherence scoring.
  • Best number of topics ≈ 11.
  • Topics matched TF-IDF and WordCloud (Loki, Hulk, Iron Man, Wanda, Vision, Spider-Man).

Sentiment Analysis#

Pipeline: length limit → remove punctuation/stopwords → lemmatization → sentiment model.

  • Submissions: Positive > Negative across weekdays.
  • Comments: Same trend, strongest engagement on Fri–Sat.
  • Time of day: Night & morning = more positive; afternoon & evening = lower.

Key Questions Answered#

1. Do fans prefer old MCU characters?
Not necessarily. Wanda & Spider-Man dominate mentions.

3. Do minorities/diverse heroes impact MCU?
Yes — WandaVision & Black Widow show positive sentiment, indicating market potential.

WandaVision (Disney+) was well-received. Deadpool also strong. Black Widow negative (linked to Johansson–Disney conflict).
