Spark Application: MCU Market & Strategy

Over a decade ago, the release of Iron Man sparked a change in the cinematic industry. The film, based on Marvel Comics, was quickly embraced by fans and led to the creation of the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) by The Walt Disney Company. In 2009, Disney acquired Marvel for $4 billion, introducing a host of other superheroes, such as Captain America and Thor, through trilogies and sagas. Disney has created different phases and universes within the MCU to continue producing new movies and generate revenue. This has contributed to the company’s collective grossed revenue of 21 billion dollars, according to Forbes.

With a diverse range of superheroes to choose from, the public has varying opinions on each character, influenced by factors such as the actor portraying them, their gender, and their powers. One of the most famous heroes, Spider-Man, was originally introduced by Sony Pictures, who bought the copyright from Marvel Comics in 1999. However, the character has since been reintroduced by MCU in multiple movies.

To answer the question of the impact of The Walt Disney Company on the entertainment industry, it is necessary to analyze the stock price, actors’ popularity, and online streaming.

Tools & Technique#

Use Spark on Azure Databricks with Python to analyze, transform, and process data, producing analytical datasets.

Data Sources

  • Reddit posts related to Marvel Studios
  • Disney stock prices

Data Preprocessing

  • Keyword extraction using regex
  • Data quality checks
  • Standardization and transformations
  • Creation of dummy variables

Analytical Methods

  • EDA: Capture interesting patterns and insights
  • ML: Sentiment analysis and predictive analysis
  • NLP: Keyword extraction and topic modeling

Features#

  • Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA): Visualizes user behavior such as post length, submission times, and discussion frequency.
  • Machine Learning: Predicts Disney stock price trends and analyzes post sentiment patterns using ML models.
  • Natural Language Processing (NLP): Performs keyword extraction, topic modeling, and sentiment analysis on popular characters.
  • Interactive Visualizations: Generates charts and dashboards to summarize insights from data.
  • Business Insights: Provides analysis of MCU fan engagement and market trends to guide potential business strategies.
