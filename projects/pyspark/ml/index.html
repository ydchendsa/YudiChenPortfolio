Machine Learning

Executive Summary#

Sentiment analysis provides insights into the mindset of Reddit users, or Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) fans. This analysis focuses on main submissions rather than comments because some comments may be unrelated to the initial post.

The Bag of Words (BOW) model predicts more positive reviews than negative or neutral, suggesting that from January 2021 to April 2022, MCU business strategy was on track. Stock price is another indicator of company performance. Netflix stock was used to train the model because Disney+ and Netflix share similar market variables. The actual Disney stock price was higher than predicted due to Disney’s broader portfolio (parks, products) and the early 2021 Covid recovery.

Business Questions Answered:

  • GOAL 1: Do Marvel Cinematic Universe launches positively correlate with Walt Disney stock price?
  • GOAL 2: Does Reddit comment popularity affect Marvel Cinematic Universe movies’ box office revenue?

Analysis Report#

BOW for Main Post Sentiment Prediction#

Using the Keras TextVectorization layer with ngrams, outputs were converted to “multi_hot” vectors. The custom Keras model includes three Dense layers and three Dropout layers. Two models were compared:

  1. Adam optimizer + Sparse Categorical Cross Entropy

    • F1 Score: 0.9890
    • AUC Score: 0.9895

  2. Adam optimizer + Mean Squared Error

    • F1 Score: 0.3533
    • AUC Score: 0.4438

Sparse Categorical Cross Entropy outperforms MSE, providing accurate sentiment predictions.

Disney Stock Price Prediction Using Keras#

A Keras model predicted Disney’s stock price using 10 years of Netflix stock data. The model achieved:

  • R-Squared: 0.9899
  • RMSE: 3.16

The model uses Huber Loss, which is less sensitive to outliers than MSE. Actual Disney stock exceeded predictions from February to April 2021 due to Covid recovery and MCU announcements.

Stock Trend Prediction Using Random Forest Pipeline#

Variables included in the analysis:

  • Number of submissions per day
  • Number of comments
  • Submission and comment length
  • Number of positive, negative, and neutral comments
  • Submission and comment scores
  • Submission weekday and sentiment (one-hot encoded)

Stock trend definition: “up” if today’s price > previous day, “down” otherwise. Weekends and holidays excluded.

Random Forest Models Comparison:

Model Accuracy ROC AUC
model1 (max_depth=5; num_trees=100) 0.725 0.705 0.784
model2 (max_depth=10; num_trees=100) 0.922 0.916 0.948
model3 (max_depth=10; num_trees=500) 0.923 0.917 0.949

Model 3 performs best. Confusion matrix shows “down” predictions are stronger, indicating negative sentiment may correlate more with stock trends. ROC and AUC > 0.9 confirms the model’s predictive power.

Feature Importance Insights:

Top 5 important variables: submission length, count of positive/neutral sentiments, comment sentiment, and weekday of submission. Positive sentiments correlate more with stock trends. Longer submissions also predict trends.

Observing peak periods (e.g., Feb–Apr 2021), higher text length and positive sentiment indicate increased investor confidence, likely contributing to stock price growth. The classification model could potentially support investment decisions in Disney stock.
