MCU Potential Future Market and Business Strategy Analysis

Conclusion#

Using natural language processing (NLP) techniques on the Reddit dataset provided valuable insights into the behaviors and preferences of users on Marvel related Reddit data. One of the most interesting findings was the popularity of Wanda, a female character in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), who was the most frequently mentioned character in the Reddit forum. This conclusion was reached by performing TF-IDF (Term Frequency - Inverse Document Frequency) and sentiment analysis on the dataset. Prior to the analysis, it was commonly assumed that the most popular MCU characters would be members of the Avengers. However, the data showed otherwise, highlighting the value of using NLP methods in understanding user behavior and challenging assumptions.

The popularity of Wanda as a female character in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), highlights the potential value of introducing more diverse gender and ethnicity into the franchise. As one of the few female characters in the overall MCU superhero league and the second female character to have her own show, Wanda serves as a role model for younger fans. The sentiment analysis of the Reddit dataset also showed that female characters tend to receive more positive reviews than male characters. This suggests that increasing diversity in the MCU could help the franchise appeal to a wider global audience. Expanding the timeline of the Reddit dataset and performing further analysis could provide additional insights into the potential benefits of diversity in the MCU.

While the stock price is often considered the best indicator of a company’s performance, it is important to note that the Walt Disney Company, which owns the MCU franchise, has a diverse portfolio of products across different industries. Therefore, using the MCU product timeline, Reddit forum submission and comment timeline, and sentiment analysis can provide valuable insights into the potential correlation between the performance of the MCU franchise and the overall performance of the Walt Disney Company’s stock. By analyzing this data, it may be possible to identify trends and relationships that can inform decision-making and help to improve the performance of the MCU and the Walt Disney Company as a whole.

According to observations, Machine Learning models can be used to predict the price of Disney using other companies with similar business strategies, such as Netflix. After Netflix decided to produce their own content, Disney followed suit and announced plans to launch their own online streaming platform. A Keras model was executed to predict Disney’s price from early 2021 to August 2022, and the results showed that the actual price was slightly higher than predicted in early 2021.

This increase in price can be attributed to Marvel Studios’ announcement of their production plan for the next phase of the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), which boosted investor confidence in the Walt Disney Company. The success of Marvel Studios is crucial to Disney’s market competitiveness and overall performance. Additionally, the confidence level in the Covid-19 recovery may also have played a role in the increased price, although this variable was not included in the prediction model.

Further Improvements#

If more time were given, the project could be expanded to include a wider timeline for the Reddit dataset. This would allow for a deeper analysis of minority subjects and the potential impact on the stock market. In order to accomplish this, additional data from other platforms such as Twitter could be collected and analyzed using natural language processing (NLP) and sentiment analysis techniques. This would provide insights into how new minority lead characters are affecting stock prices and whether there is a correlation between the two.

Another potential area for expansion is the inclusion of data related to the COVID-19 pandemic. The pandemic may have had an impact on people’s viewing choices and could provide valuable insights into the success of certain MCU productions. Additionally, the current dataset is limited to the time period between early 2021 and August 2022, during which the MCU was not consistently releasing new products due to the ongoing pandemic. Expanding the dataset to include data from before 2021 or after 2022 would provide a wider range of topics to analyze and potentially yield more valuable insights.

One of the initial goals of the project was to analyze the correlation between Reddit discussions and MCU box office prices. However, due to time constraints, insufficient box office price information could not be found and utilized for the analysis. With more time, it may be possible to gather this information and conduct a more comprehensive analysis.
